          DMARC Phased Deployment Roadmap & Policy Staging
        

        

          Industry best practices for transitioning your domain from initial monitoring to full spoofing enforcement.
        


        
        


          
          

            

              

                Phase 1
                p=none
              

              
Monitoring & Discovery

              

                Publish a monitoring policy. Email delivery is never blocked. Review daily RUA aggregate XML reports to discover all legitimate third-party senders (Salesforce, SendGrid, Zendesk, etc.).
              

              

                v=DMARC1; p=none; pct=100; rua=mailto:dmarc-rua@example.com;
              

            

            

              Goal: Complete sender audit & fix SPF/DKIM misconfigurations.
            

          


          
          

            

              

                Phase 2
                p=quarantine
              

              
Quarantine & Soft Enforcement

              

                Once legitimate senders are aligned, divert spoofed messages to spam/quarantine. Senders can gradually ramp percentage (e.g. pct=25, pct=50, pct=100).
              

              

                v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; pct=100; rua=mailto:dmarc-rua@example.com;
              

            

            

              Goal: Neutralize spoofing in user inboxes without hard drops.
            

          


          
          

            

              

                Phase 3
                p=reject
              

              
Full Rejection & Brand Protection

              

                Receiving mail gateways will outright reject and drop unauthorized emails during SMTP transaction. Zero phishing emails reach users.
              

              

                v=DMARC1; p=reject; pct=100; rua=mailto:dmarc-rua@example.com; aspf=r; adkim=r;
              

            

            

              Goal: Complete cryptographic immunity to domain spoofing.
